Two indices. The full AQI applies CPCB's National AQI formula to all six modelled pollutants. Modelled ozone sets it on most days, including in coastal cities, so the charts lead with the PM index: the same formula on PM2.5 and PM10 only. Neither is the official CPCB figure, which comes from ground stations; these values are CAMS model output on a grid.

About the data. Air quality: CAMS global model via the Open-Meteo Air Quality API; weather: ERA5 reanalysis via Open-Meteo. Values are for the model grid cell nearest each city, not ground stations. AQI: CPCB National AQI (2014) breakpoints; 24-hour means for PM and NO₂/SO₂, highest 8-hour mean for O₃ and CO; complete IST days only.
